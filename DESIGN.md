# Design Style

## Style Prompt

Minimal Evidence: a restrained technical film for OpenTool Mesh. Each scene shows one proof point only, with large text, strict alignment, and almost no decoration. The viewer should remember the loop, the verification signal, and the trace/report evidence.

## Colors

- Background: `#101214`
- Text: `#F4F1E8`
- Muted: `#8D96A0`
- Accent: `#FFB300`

## Typography

- Inter or Helvetica-style sans for titles.
- JetBrains Mono or IBM Plex Mono for CLI fields, hashes, and URIs.

## Motion

- One idea per scene.
- Hard cuts or precise zooms.
- Reveal key fields one at a time.
- Keep callouts short and oversized.

## What NOT to Do

- No gradient-heavy AI look.
- No dense diagrams.
- No more than two callouts per frame.
- No fake terminal noise or decorative glitch.
